Kingshot Beginner Guide: TC1 to TG1

Complete roadmap by MRXGAMER covering rushing Town Center, daily habits, milestones, and Truegold 1.

Read Full Guide

1. The Golden Rule: Rush Your Town Center
• Do NOT waste time maxing out every building early.
• Only upgrade the exact buildings required for the next Town Center (TC) level.
• Keep Construction, Research, and Troop Training queues busy 24/7.

2. Join a Top Alliance Immediately (At TC 6)
• Unlock the Embassy at TC 8 and join the most active alliance.
• Tap "Help" on every upgrade so teammates cut your build timers.
• Teleport near alliance territory for safety and resource bonuses.

3. Daily Habits for Fast Growth
• Complete all Daily Missions for free gems, speedups, and hero shards.
• Clear Watchtower Intel missions whenever they refresh.
• Gather resources constantly and never let stamina cap at 100%.

4. Smart Upgrade & Research Order
• Priority: Town Center → Embassy → Academy → Troop Camps.
• Academy: Focus on "Growth" (Construction & Research Speed) first.
• Save speedups and backpack resources for major events like KvK.

5. Key Level Milestones
• TC 6: Alliance | TC 9: Academy | TC 15: Hero Gear | TC 20: Mastery Forging | TC 22: Governor Gear | TC 25: Charms | TC 30: Max Standard Level.

6. Crossing From TC 30 to Truegold 1 (TG1)
• Around server day 60–70, the Age of Truegold begins.
• Complete 4 sub-stages (30-1 to 30-4) requiring Embassy & Academy Lv. 30.
• Total Cost: 528 Truegold (132 per stage) plus massive Bread, Wood, Stone, and Iron.
• After TG1: Rally leaders upgrade Command Center next; rally joiners upgrade Barracks or Range first.
